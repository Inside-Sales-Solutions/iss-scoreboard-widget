Using the scoreboard

The scoreboard shows how every SDR and BDR is tracking against the targets for their level. It reads Zoho CRM live each time you open the Scoreboard tab, so an appointment or call you log shows up the next time the board loads.

01

Getting around

Open the Scoreboard tab in Zoho CRM. The header shows when the data was loaded. Select Refresh to load it again without leaving the page.

Pick a time period

The buttons at the top right change the period for the whole board: Today, This week, This month and Last month. Targets shrink to match: "This month" on the 10th measures you against the working days so far, not the whole month.

Switch views

  • Leaderboard: the main board with totals, rankings and each rep's scorecard.
  • Competitions: any contests that are running, with standings. The number on the button is how many are live.
  • Daily digest: a recap of the last working day and who needs what to finish the month on target.
Draft notice

A DRAFT banner means the targets for that group (SDR, BDR or Trainee) haven't been confirmed yet. The scores still work; the targets behind them may change once an admin saves the group's Board.

02

The Leaderboard view

Read it from top to bottom: team totals, the top 3, then everyone.

  • Totals: scheduled appointments, attended appointments, scheduled points, attended points, calls and active reps for the period.
  • Top of the board: the 3 highest Activity Scores.
  • Leaderboard: every rep ranked by Activity Score. Use BDR, P4P, Training or All to choose the division. "Att. points" shows actual against target, for example 3.6 / 20.
  • Division standings: the average Activity Score of each division.
  • How the scores work: the rules and the weights each group uses, at the bottom of the page.

Rep scorecards

  1. Select a rep's row on the leaderboard. Their scorecard opens underneath.
  2. Each KPI shows actual against target with a bar and a status: On pace (100% or more), Close (70% to 99%) or Behind (under 70%).
  3. Objective score (month) is the share of working days this month the rep hit both the daily calls target and the daily scheduled target.
  4. The scorecard also shows how many points the rep needs for the next level. Select the row again to close it.

Race to the next level

Promotion is based on the average attended points over the last 3 full months. Each bar shows that average against the next level up, for example 12.4 → 20. Levels never go down once reached. The top 15 show first; select Show all to see everyone.

03

Competitions

Live competitions show first. Use Active, Ended or All to see past ones.

  • Individual contests rank each rep.
  • Head-to-head teams put teams (Team A, Team B and so on) against each other. A team's score is either the average per rep or the team total, as set on the contest.
  • Teams by division puts whole divisions against each other.

Each card can be measured on the Activity Score or on up to 3 KPIs. Open How the scores are worked out on any card to see each rep's numbers against their targets.

04

Daily digest

The digest covers the last working day, so on a Monday it shows Friday.

  • Yesterday's stars: the top 3 in each division for scheduled appointments, attended appointments and scheduled points.
  • Biggest climbers: whose month-to-date pace moved most after yesterday.
  • Closing in on promotion: reps on course to lift their 3-month average to the next level by month end.
  • Within reach of pace: reps between 50% and 70% of pace, with how many bookings a day they need to finish on target.
  • Competitions: a quick look at anything live.
05

How the Activity Score works

Your targets depend on your level (your "Payband Milestone" in the CRM), so 100 means on pace whatever your level.

  1. For each KPI, divide your actual by your target for the period so far.
  2. Cap each result at 150%, so one huge number can't carry the whole score.
  3. Weight the results. Each group (SDR, BDR, Trainee) has its own weights, listed under "How the scores work" at the bottom of the board.
  4. Add them up. 100 = on pace, over 100 is ahead.
KPIWhat counts
Scheduled appointmentsEvery meeting booked, on the day it was booked, unless it's now Cancelled
Attended appointmentsAttended, Reschedule - ABD or Cancelled - ISS, on the meeting date
Attended pointsPoints on attended appointments. The monthly target is your level number (Avg 24 = 24 points)
Scheduled pointsPoints on every meeting booked, on the day it was booked
First encountersBooked meetings marked First Encounter
Senior contactsBooked meetings with a Director, Vice President or Chief-level contact
CallsCalls logged. The calls target is per day and the same at every level
ReferralsCalls logged with the result "Suggested Colleague"

Working days are Monday to Friday, minus any holidays an admin has added. Days run on Eastern time.

07

Scoreboard Admin

For users with the Administrator profile. Select Scoreboard Admin at the top right of the board. Changes show on the board the next time it loads.

The page has 3 tabs: Boards, Contests and Holidays. Select Back to scoreboard to return.

Boards: everyday targets and weights

There is one Board each for SDR, BDR and Trainee. The main scoreboard always uses these numbers. A contest's own numbers only apply inside that contest.

  1. Choose the group at the top: SDR, BDR or Trainee.
  2. Scoring weights: how much each KPI counts toward the Activity Score. Weights are relative and don't need to add up to 100. A KPI with weight 0 is shown but not scored.
  3. Cap on any one KPI: 150 means no extra credit past 150% of a target.
  4. Level targets: one row per Payband Milestone. Calls are per day; the rest are per month and pro-rated by working day. Leave a box blank to use the starting rule shown in grey.
  5. Rep overrides: to give one rep different targets (ramping up, reduced hours), pick the rep, fill in only the boxes that change, add a reason, then select Apply override.
  6. Select Save. Saving also confirms the targets, which removes the DRAFT banner for that group.

Contests

The list on the left shows contests by Active, Upcoming, Ended or All, with Edit, Copy and Delete. Delete asks you to select it a second time.

  1. Name the contest and pick the Group: SDR, BDR, Trainee or All (for contests across divisions).
  2. Measured on: the Activity Score, or 1 to 3 KPIs.
  3. Scoring: "% of own target" (fair across levels), "Raw total" (most wins), or "% of fixed target" (everyone chases the same number).
  4. Format: Individual, Head-to-head teams, or Teams by division (needs Group set to All). For Individual, choose the whole group or pick the reps.
  5. For teams, add reps to Team A and Team B (up to Team F with Add another team) and choose the team score: average per rep or team total.
  6. When: Today, This week, This month or Custom dates. Add a goal and prize if you like.
  7. Targets and weights are copied from the group's Board when the form opens. Change them if this contest should be different, then select Save.
Tip

To run last month's contest again, select Copy on it, change the dates and save.

Holidays

Dates listed here are not working days. Daily targets, the digest and contests all skip them.

  1. Select Add holiday, then enter the date and a name (for example "Thanksgiving").
  2. Select Save. A date that falls on a weekend is allowed but doesn't change anything.
08

Common questions

Why is everything 0 on "Today"?

Nothing has been logged yet today, or it's a weekend or holiday. On the 1st of the month the board opens on "Last month" for the same reason.

I booked a meeting but my number didn't change.

The board reads the CRM when it loads. Select Refresh. Scheduled appointments count on the day they were booked; attended ones count on the meeting date once the status changes.

My level or division looks wrong.

Your level comes from "Payband Milestone" and your division from "Team" on your CRM user record. Ask an admin to correct them there.

Why don't I see other divisions?

Reps see their own division on the leaderboard and digest. Division standings and competitions show everyone.
